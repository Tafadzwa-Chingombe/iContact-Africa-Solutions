Privacy · Terms · Cookies

The short version

We don’t use cookies on the public website, we don’t use advertising trackers, and we don’t track you across other websites. We count visits anonymously (see below) and store only what’s needed for the site and the Client Portal to work. That’s why you don’t see a cookie banner.

What we store

Name / typeWherePurposeHow long
Supabase login session (sb-…-auth-token, local storage)Client Portal onlyKeeps you signed in to the portal securely. Strictly necessary.Until you sign out or the session expires
Visit counter (ic_sid, session storage)Public websiteA random number so one visit is counted once and we can see how many people are on the site right now. Not linked to you, no IP address stored, not shared. Skipped if your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control.Until you close the tab
Portal preferences (session storage)Client Portal onlyRemembers small display choices, such as a list filter, while the tab is open.Until you close the tab

The public website itself sets no cookies.

Third parties

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and code libraries from jsDelivr; these services receive your IP address to deliver the files but don’t set cookies through our site. Links to WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn take you to those platforms, which use their own cookies under their own policies.

Your choices

You can clear or block cookies and site storage in your browser settings at any time. If you block storage for the Client Portal, you won’t be able to stay signed in.

Changes

If we ever add marketing tools or analytics that use cookies, we’ll update this policy and ask for your consent first where the law requires it. Questions?
