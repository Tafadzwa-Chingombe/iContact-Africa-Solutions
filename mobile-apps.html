Mobile Apps

Put your business in your customers’ pockets.

Android and iPhone apps, and app-like websites that install to the home screen without an app store, designed, built and published for you.

What apps can do

Built for what your customers do on their phones.

We help you choose the features that matter, and leave out the ones that only add cost.

Orders & bookings

Let customers order, book or reorder in a few taps, with confirmations sent automatically.

Payments

Take payments in the app with providers such as EcoCash, Paynow or cards, where the provider supports it.

Notifications

Send order updates, reminders and offers straight to your customers’ phones.

Staff & field apps

Give your team an app for deliveries, site visits, stock checks or job sheets.

Choose your type of app

Two ways to get on your customers’ phones.

App-like website (PWA)

Lower cost and fastest to launch
  • Installs from your website to the home screen
  • Works on Android and iPhone
  • No app-store approval needed
  • Updates go live instantly

Android & iPhone app

Best for a full app-store presence
  • Listed on Google Play and the App Store
  • One codebase for both platforms
  • Push notifications and device features
  • We handle the store submissions

Not sure which you need? We’ll recommend one on the scoping call, based on your customers and budget.
