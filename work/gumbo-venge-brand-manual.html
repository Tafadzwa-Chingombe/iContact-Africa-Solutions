Corporate Profile Design

TOTUS: a corporate profile for a complete law firm.

A 15-page corporate profile for Gumbo-Venge Law Attorneys (Totus Law) — the firm, its vision and values, services, and every lawyer on the team.

The challenge

What Gumbo-Venge Law Attorneys needed

Gumbo-Venge Law Attorneys is a law firm in Mount Pleasant, Harare, guided by “Totus” — a philosophy of comprehensive, holistic legal solutions. Its vision is to be “a premier law chambers recognized for shaping jurisprudence” and influencing the evolution of the law.

The firm needed a corporate profile that introduces that philosophy, sets out its services and presents a growing team of partners and associates — in a format that looks as premium as the firm’s brand.

Our approach

How we did it

  1. Built on the TOTUS brandThe Gumbo-Venge shield logo and the “Totus Law” lock-up sit on every slide, with the firm’s gold-and-black palette carried through headings, labels and rules for instant recognition.
  2. Firm, vision and values up frontClear slides for the firm, its vision, aim and mission, and five values — Client Impact, Strategic Excellence, Client Partnership, Professional Discipline, and Independence & Integrity — paired with dramatic legal imagery.
  3. Services at a glanceCorporate law, litigation, conveyancing, labour law and other services summarised in one slide, with quick-reference labels.
  4. A slide for every lawyerConsistent profile cards for each partner and associate, with portrait, title, biography and direct phone and email, followed by the support team and a careers invitation for future talent.

The outcome

What they got

  • A premium 15-page profile in the firm’s own brand
  • Every lawyer introduced with credentials and contacts
  • Services and values explained at a glance
  • Widescreen format for screens, email and presentations

Deliverables

  • 15-page corporate profile
  • Cover & firm overview
  • Vision, mission & values slides
  • Services overview
  • Lawyer profile layouts
  • Support staff & careers pages
  • Presentation-ready PDF
