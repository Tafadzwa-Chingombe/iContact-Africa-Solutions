The challenge

What Parker Paints needed

Parker Paints makes durable, high-quality paints for Zimbabwean homes, businesses and industrial spaces. The old website didn’t reflect the quality of the brand or make it easy to explore the range.

The redesign needed to showcase colours and products clearly and turn visitors — homeowners, contractors and dealers — into enquiries.

Our approach

How we did it

  1. Product-first structureOrganised the site around products and a colour catalogue so the right paint is a couple of taps away.
  2. Useful toolsA paint calculator and a clear “Find a Dealer” route so visitors can go from browsing to buying.
  3. Colour-led designA bold, image-rich design that lets the colours do the selling, built to load fast on mobile data.
  4. Answers up frontAn FAQ and clear contact paths to cut down repetitive phone calls.

The outcome

What they got

  • A modern site that matches the brand
  • Colours and products easy to browse on any device
  • Clear routes from browsing to a dealer
  • Fewer repetitive questions thanks to the FAQ

Deliverables

  • Website redesign
  • Colour catalogue
  • Paint calculator
  • Dealer finder
  • FAQ
  • Mobile-first build
