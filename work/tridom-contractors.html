The challenge

What Tridom Contractors needed

Tridom Contractors delivers construction, civil works and infrastructure development across Zimbabwe. Clients and tender committees want proof of capability before they pick up the phone.

The site had to look established and credible, show completed projects at a glance and make it easy to request a quote.

Our approach

How we did it

  1. Capabilities firstStructured around services and sectors so tender teams find what they need quickly.
  2. Proof up frontKey numbers and completed projects front and centre to build trust immediately.
  3. Strong, corporate designA dark, confident design language that signals scale and reliability.
  4. Quote requests everywhereA prominent “Get a free quote” route on every page.

The outcome

What they got

  • A professional site to include in tender submissions
  • Completed projects showcased clearly
  • Easy quote requests from any page
  • Built to grow with new projects
